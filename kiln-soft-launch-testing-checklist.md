# Kiln — Soft Launch Testing Checklist

Thanks for helping test Kiln! 🔥 Please test on **your own phone** if you can — that's how most people will use it. Try it both **logged out** and **logged in**.

If anything breaks or confuses you, **grab a screenshot** and send it back with a quick note. Small details help a lot.

---

## ⏱️ First 60 seconds
- [ ] The feed loads quickly
- [ ] Videos start playing automatically **with sound**
- [ ] I can tell what Kiln is and who it's for
- [ ] I know what to tap next (I don't feel lost)

## 📱 Browsing the feed
- [ ] Videos autoplay smoothly — no black screens or frozen clips
- [ ] Sound is crisp and starts right away
- [ ] Like, Save, Comment, and Follow all feel instant and work
- [ ] Scrolling between posts feels smooth

## 🔍 Discover page
- [ ] "Trending now" shows real, interesting content
- [ ] "New & rising artists" makes me want to check someone out
- [ ] "On a streak" feels lively
- [ ] I tapped into an artist I didn't know before

## 👤 Artist profiles
- [ ] It's clear how to Follow, Shop, or Support the artist
- [ ] Their posts, shop items, and info all load correctly

## 🎨 For artists (if you make things)
- [ ] I posted a video or photo and the upload was smooth
- [ ] My post appears in my profile and the feed, with sound
- [ ] The "Tips to get seen" hint was helpful (not annoying)
- [ ] I set up a shop listing / drop / workshop without trouble

## 🛒 For buyers
- [ ] Adding to cart and checking out was clear
- [ ] The address step made sense
- [ ] I got a confirmation and a receipt by email
- [ ] Bidding on an auction or joining a drop waitlist felt live

---

## 💬 After testing — please answer these 3
1. **What confused you or made you stop?**

2. **What made you want to come back?**

3. **Would you tell an artist friend about Kiln? Why or why not?**
