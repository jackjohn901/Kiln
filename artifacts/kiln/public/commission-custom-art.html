Commission Custom Handmade Art

Sometimes you need something made specifically for you — a ceramic set in a specific glaze, a blown glass vessel in exact dimensions, a woven piece in a particular color palette, or a forged metal object for a specific purpose. Kiln's commission system connects you directly with the craft artist who can make it, handles the contract and milestone payments, and keeps everything accountable from first message to delivery.

How commissioning on Kiln works

1
Find your artist — Browse Discover by technique and style. Watch their process videos. Read their past work. Every artist shows you exactly what they make and how they make it before you commit.
2
Submit your request — Describe what you want: dimensions, material, color palette, finish, function, deadline, and budget. Upload reference images if helpful.
3
Receive a quote — The artist reviews your request and responds with a quote and a milestone schedule. You can message back and forth before agreeing.
4
Approve milestones — Payment is released milestone by milestone as the work progresses. You see work-in-progress photos at each stage and approve before the next phase begins.
5
Receive your piece — Final payment released on delivery. Your commission comes with full provenance and the artist's process documentation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I commission custom handmade art?

On Kiln: find an artist whose work matches your vision, submit a commission request with your specifications, receive a quote and milestone schedule, approve it, and payment releases milestone by milestone as work progresses. Visit kilndrop.com/kiln/discover to browse artists accepting commissions.

Where can I commission custom ceramics?

Kiln is the best place to commission custom ceramics. Browse ceramic artists by technique and location, watch their process videos to see their aesthetic, then submit a commission request. Artists on Kiln specialize in wheel-thrown functional ware, hand-built sculptural ceramics, raku-fired pieces, and more.

How long does a commission take?

Timelines vary by discipline and complexity. A ceramic piece typically takes 6-10 weeks (including drying, bisque firing, glazing, and final firing). Blown glass pieces often take 2-4 weeks. Woven textiles can take 4-12 weeks depending on size. Each artist specifies their timeline in their commission rates, and the milestone system keeps the process transparent throughout.

What's the difference between commissioning and buying ready-made work?

Ready-made work on Kiln's shop is available immediately — you buy it and it ships. A commission is made specifically for you from scratch. Commissions take longer but allow you to specify exactly what you want: dimensions, materials, glaze colors, fiber type, metal finish, wood species — every detail. The piece exists because you asked for it.
