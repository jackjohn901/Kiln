Kiln Launches Creator Platform Built Exclusively for Craft Artists — Combining Process Video, Sales, Workshops, and Patron Subscriptions in One Place

New platform gives ceramicists, glassblowers, weavers, metalworkers, and woodworkers a complete creative business toolkit — and gives collectors a feed that makes buying handmade work as natural as watching TikTok.

PORTLAND, OR, May 21, 2025 — Kiln, a new creator marketplace and social platform, today announced its public launch at kilndrop.com/kiln/. The platform is built exclusively for craft artists across all hand-skill disciplines — ceramics, glassblowing, weaving, metalwork, woodworking, and more — and combines every tool a working craft artist needs to build a creative business: a TikTok-style process video feed, a direct sales shop, workshop booking, patron subscriptions, custom commission management, limited-edition drops, and live auctions.

"The craft world has been force-fit into platforms that were never built for it," said the Kiln team. "Instagram gives you visibility but no sales. Etsy gives you sales but no story. Patreon gives you subscriptions but no shop. Skillshare gives you students but no community. Every craft artist ends up stitching five platforms together and still falling through the gaps. Kiln puts everything in one place — with a craft-only audience already there."

"We built Kiln because we believe the next generation of craft collectors doesn't want to browse a grid of products — they want to watch a ceramicist pull a piece off the wheel, see the glaze develop in the kiln, and buy it directly from the person who made it. The process is the product."

Kiln's feed works like TikTok but with a craft-specific audience. Artists post short-form process videos — wheel throwing, glassblowing at the furnace, warping a floor loom, forging iron — and viewers discover their shop, book their workshops, and become monthly patrons from the same interface. The platform also features technique-based guild communities, a mentorship program, an opportunities board with open residencies and grant listings, a built-in AI grant writer, and an AI craft assistant that answers technical questions about materials and process.

The platform launches with support for every major revenue stream craft artists use: direct shop sales with no listing fees, limited-edition drops with built-in waitlists, live bidding auctions on one-of-a-kind work, in-person and online workshop booking, multiple patron subscription tiers, milestone-based custom commissions, and tips. For collectors, Kiln provides a Collector Portal with certificate of authenticity records, provenance tracking, and a wishlist.

Kiln is free to join for artists and collectors. The platform is available now at kilndrop.com/kiln/.

About Kiln
Kiln is a creator marketplace and social platform built exclusively for craft artists. It combines short-form process videos, direct sales, workshop booking, patron subscriptions, custom commissions, limited drops, and live auctions in one purpose-built platform. Kiln serves ceramicists, glassblowers, weavers, metalworkers, woodworkers, and every other hand-skill discipline. Available free at kilndrop.com/kiln/.
