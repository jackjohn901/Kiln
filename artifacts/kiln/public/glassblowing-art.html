Glass techniques on Kiln

  • Glassblowing (hot glass) — furnace work at 2,100°F, gather to finished form. The most dramatic and physical glass discipline.
  • Flameworking / Lampworking — borosilicate and soft glass worked at the torch. Intricate details, color, and precision impossible in hot glass.
  • Kiln forming (fused glass) — sheets and frit assembled and fused in a kiln. Slumping, casting, and architectural glass work.
  • Pâte de verre — ground glass packed into a mold and fired. The most painterly of all glass techniques.
  • Art glass chandelier & lighting — blown and cast glass used in functional lighting design.
  • Glass sculpture — architectural scale and gallery works combining multiple hot and cold glass techniques.
