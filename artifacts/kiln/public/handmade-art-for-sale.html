How buying on Kiln is different

When you buy on Kiln, you know exactly who made the piece. Every artist has a profile with process videos — you can watch them make the kind of work you're about to buy. You understand the materials, the time, the skill. You're not buying a product. You're buying a relationship with a maker.

Every purchase comes with provenance: the artist's name, technique, materials, and a link to their profile. For significant works, you can request a Certificate of Authenticity through the Collector Portal.

Limited drops and live auctions

For the most sought-after works, Kiln offers two additional buying formats:

  • Drops — limited quantities of new work released at a specific time. Join the waitlist to be notified. Patron subscribers get early access.
  • Auctions — real-time bidding on one-of-a-kind pieces. Set a bid and Kiln notifies you if you're outbid.

Commission custom handmade art

Can't find exactly what you're looking for? Commission it. Describe the piece — dimensions, materials, colors, function — and receive quotes from craft artists who specialize in exactly that. Milestone-based payment means you approve each stage before funds are released.

The aesthetic trends driving handmade art right now

Buyers are increasingly seeking: organic handmade decor, imperfect textures and forms, sculptural home objects, handmade luxury, folk-inspired craft, emotionally resonant design, and pieces with a visible human hand. Kiln is where all of those things live — made by working craft artists who have dedicated their practice to exactly these aesthetics.
