What you get on Kiln

  • A direct shop — list original works and sell directly to buyers, no middlemen
  • Process video feed — share your making process and grow an audience of people who love what you make and how you make it
  • Workshop booking — offer in-person or online classes and take bookings directly
  • Patron subscriptions — monthly support tiers so collectors can back you consistently
  • Commission requests — take custom orders with milestone-based payment management
  • Limited drops — release work at a set date and time with countdown timers and waitlists
  • Live auctions — run real-time bidding on your most exceptional pieces
  • Analytics — see post performance, follower growth, and audience data
  • Grant writing AI — generate compelling grant applications from your artist statement
  • Newsletter — send updates to your subscriber list

Who is Kiln for?

Kiln is built for working craft artists across all hand-skill disciplines:

  • Ceramicists and potters (functional and sculptural)
  • Glassblowers, flameworkers, and kiln formers
  • Weavers, textile artists, and natural dyers
  • Blacksmiths and metalworkers
  • Woodworkers, turners, and furniture makers
  • Enamelists and jewelry makers
  • Lost-wax casters and bronze sculptors
  • Any artist whose work involves mastering a hand skill and a material

The shift that makes Kiln matter now

Buyers are no longer just looking for objects. They want to know who made it, how it was made, and why it matters. The strongest trend in the creative economy is the shift from buying products to buying identity, story, and process.

Kiln is built for this. The process video feed is the center of the experience — not a shop grid. When buyers watch you make something, they don't just want to own the object. They want to keep watching. That's how you build a real audience and a sustainable craft business.
