Craft Process Videos — The Making Is the Content

Kiln is a TikTok-style video feed dedicated entirely to craft process content. Every post is from a real craft artist sharing how they make their work — from raw material to finished object. Pottery throwing, glassblowing at the furnace, warping a floor loom, forging iron at the anvil, woodworking joinery, textile dyeing, metalcasting — all of it, in short-form vertical video.

What you'll see on Kiln's process feed

🏺 Pottery & Ceramics

Wheel throwing, trimming, glazing, kiln loading, raku firing, the bisque reveal — all the satisfying stages of making a ceramic object.

🔥 Glassblowing

Gathering molten glass at 2,100°F, blowing, color application, marvering, shaping, and annealing. One of the most visually compelling processes in craft.

🧶 Weaving & Fiber

Warping a floor loom, beating weft, natural dyeing sequences, tapestry in progress, spinning raw wool — meditative and precise.

⚒ Blacksmithing & Metalwork

Forging steel at the forge, hammer work, finishing and quenching. One of the most visceral and dramatic craft processes to watch.

🪵 Woodworking

Hand-tool joinery, lathe turning, carving, finishing — the quiet precision of working with wood grain.

🎨 Natural Dyeing

Mordanting fibers, dyebath preparation, color pulls from botanical sources — the slow chemistry of color made from plants.

🔩 Flameworking

Borosilicate rods melting at the torch, color wrapping, sculpting — intricate detail work in hot glass.

✨ Enamelwork & Cloisonné

Laying copper wire, packing vitreous enamel, firing — the jeweler's art where glass meets metal.

Why Kiln's process feed is different

TikTok and Instagram have plenty of craft content — but it's mixed into everything else, and the algorithm decides what you see. On Kiln, every single post is from a working craft artist.
