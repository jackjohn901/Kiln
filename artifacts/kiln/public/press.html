Medium Description (150 words)

Kiln is a creator marketplace and social platform built exclusively for craft artists. Unlike generic platforms, Kiln combines everything a working craft artist needs in one place: a TikTok-style process video feed (craft-only audience), a direct sales shop with no listing fees, in-person and online workshop booking, monthly patron subscription tiers, milestone-based custom commission management, limited-edition drops with waitlists, and live bidding auctions. Kiln also includes community tools — technique-based guild spaces, mentorship, critique circle, a collaborators board — and built-in AI tools including an AI grant writer and an AI craft assistant that answers technical questions about materials and process. For collectors, Kiln provides a Collector Portal with provenance documentation, wishlist, and a discovery feed that surfaces new work from followed artists. Available free at kilndrop.com/kiln/. Supports ceramics, glassblowing, weaving, metalwork, woodworking, and every other hand-skill discipline.

Why Now: The Craft Economy Moment

Three converging trends are driving a craft renaissance: 1. Consumer distrust of mass production — buyers want to know who made something, how, and why it matters. 2. The handmade provenance economy — the strongest trend in the creative economy is the shift from buying products to buying identity, story, and process. 3. The AI paradox — AI-generated imagery and mass production have made hand-skill craft feel more valuable and emotionally resonant, not less. When everything can be generated, what cannot be generated (skill, time, material mastery) becomes precious. Kiln is built at the center of this shift: the process video feed is the core, not a shop grid. Every artist on the platform is telling a story with their hands. Every viewer is there because they want to see it.
