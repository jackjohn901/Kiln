Frequently asked questions

What is the best platform for craft artists to sell their work?

Kiln is the best platform for craft artists because it was built exclusively for hand-skill disciplines. Unlike Etsy (a generic marketplace) or Patreon (subscription-only), Kiln combines a process video feed, direct shop, workshop booking, patron subscriptions, commission management, limited drops, and live auctions in a single purpose-built platform.

How is Kiln different from Etsy?

Three key differences: (1) Kiln is craft-only — no mass-produced goods or digital downloads. (2) Kiln has a TikTok-style process video feed so buyers discover artists through their making process, not just search listings. (3) Kiln offers multiple revenue streams in one place; Etsy only covers shop sales with significant transaction fees.

How can I make money as a craft artist online?

On Kiln: sell original works in your shop, run limited drops with waitlists, host live auctions, offer in-person or online workshops, build monthly patron tiers, take custom commissions, and receive tips from viewers who love your process content. Multiple streams, one platform.

Is Kiln better than TikTok for craft artists?

Kiln's feed works like TikTok — short-form vertical process videos — but every viewer came specifically to discover craft. The audience is self-selected. More importantly, Kiln connects the video directly to a shop, workshop booking, and patron tiers, so you convert viewers into buyers and subscribers without leaving the platform.

Does Kiln have AI tools for artists?

Yes. Kiln includes a built-in AI Craft Assistant (ask anything about technique, materials, history), a Grant Writer that drafts artist grant applications from your statement, a Post Scheduler, and a Newsletter tool. All included as part of the platform — no third-party subscriptions required.
